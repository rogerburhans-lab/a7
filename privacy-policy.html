Data Collection and Privacy Principles

At Wrist Vista Journal, we respect the privacy of our readers and watch craft candidates. This Privacy Policy details the technical details processed by this informational journal and our commitment to security.

Log Files & Server Analytics

We log basic technical details to monitor site security, server load, and basic visitor counts. These logs include IP addresses, browser types, referral paths, ISP, and date/time markers. This technical data is evaluated in aggregate and is never connected to individual identities or specific personal details.

Google Analytics

This website utilizes Google Analytics to trace anonymized visitor count and reading interactions. Google Analytics stores cookies on your browser. You can prevent the placement of cookies by checking your web browser's cookie settings.

Communications Data Protection

When contacting us via the contact form, your input is redirected using a standard mailto link to open your local email client. No contact database is maintained on our web servers. We preserve your name and message context solely to address your query and do not share these details with third parties.
